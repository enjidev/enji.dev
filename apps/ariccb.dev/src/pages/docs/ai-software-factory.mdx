AI Software Factory is a Linear-integrated workflow for moving product work from idea to implementation with less handoff friction. It is designed to feel like a native extension of Linear: the issue remains the source of truth, while AI-assisted planning, implementation support, review, QA, and progress updates happen around it.

The goal is not to replace developers. It is to give teams a clearer operating layer for turning well-scoped product intent into reviewed, testable work that stays connected to the planning system the team already uses.

---

## What it does

- Turns Linear issues and product context into clearer implementation paths.
- Coordinates AI-assisted planning, build support, review, QA, and status updates.
- Keeps developers working from their own machines instead of forcing everything into a detached cloud IDE.
- Gives teams a more native-feeling code factory experience inside Linear, where work can be queued, tracked, reviewed, and moved forward with less manual coordination.

## How it fits together

At a high level, the system has two layers:

1. **Local workers on developer devices** — each worker runs close to the developer's real environment, tools, repos, and preferences.
2. **A server-side sync layer** — the coordination layer that keeps Linear, the work queue, and the distributed workers aligned.

That split keeps the experience grounded in real development environments while still giving the team a shared workflow for assigning, monitoring, reviewing, and coordinating AI-assisted work.

## Why Linear matters

Linear is already where teams define the work. AI Software Factory builds from that premise instead of asking teams to manage another disconnected dashboard.

The Linear Agents API connection makes the workflow feel embedded: work can be framed, queued, updated, and reviewed from the same planning surface teams already trust. It creates a tighter loop between product intent, engineering execution, and team visibility.

## Product direction

AI Software Factory is designed for teams that want practical AI leverage without losing control of quality, context, or accountability.
